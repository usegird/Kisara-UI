1. Basic policy
Usegrid institute ("we") puts no mechanism into KisaraUI (the "App") to collect your personal information. The App has no servers of ours and puts no analytics, advertising or tracking into the screen. The only things we handle are the delivery logs that the delivery provider records and aggregates on our behalf (see 6) and what is sent to us in inquiries and reports, together with any files attached to them (see 11). We do not provide your information to third parties or sell it, except as required by law. The App is not intended for people under 18 or under the age of majority where they live. If we learn that we have received information from such a person through an inquiry, we will delete it.

2. Information kept on your device
Destination URLs, workflows, chats, presets, avatars, settings, downloaded Similarity models, the time you agreed to the terms and similar information stay in your browser. We cannot read them. They are erased when you delete this site's data in your browser settings. Records for investigating problems (the errors that occurred and the kinds of operations before and after them) are kept on the screen only while the page is open. They are not sent anywhere unless you export them as a diagnostics file and send it.

3. Information sent to destinations
The App sends what you enter, your settings, images and similar data only to the ComfyUI and LLM servers and services you specify, including external cloud services (the "Destinations"). When you use web search, search terms and the URLs to retrieve are sent to the Destination and to its search and retrieval providers. How the Destinations handle it is governed by their own terms and policies. Because we do not receive what you enter or generate, we do not use it to train AI models either. Whether a Destination uses what you send for training or other purposes is governed by its own terms and policies, so please check them.

4. Downloading the Similarity model
If you enable Similarity in the chat tab, the App downloads the model it uses from the site of Hugging Face, Inc. ("Hugging Face") at huggingface.co. When it does, your IP address, the URLs of the files downloaded, your browser type and similar data reach Hugging Face and are handled under Hugging Face's privacy policy, which may include handling outside your country, such as in the United States. This download is necessary to provide the Similarity feature you have enabled (for users in the EU and elsewhere, it is processing necessary for the performance of a contract). For how Hugging Face safeguards transfers outside your country, please see Hugging Face's privacy policy. Your chats are not sent to Hugging Face. The downloaded model stays in your browser and is loaded from there next time.

5. Backups in ComfyUI
If backup is enabled, while connected to ComfyUI the App writes backups of your chats, avatars, presets and similar data to the user folder (kisara/) of the ComfyUI you specify. The backups remain as files of that ComfyUI and may be readable by whoever administers it and by other people who use the same ComfyUI. On a shared server, check this before enabling backup. Clearing your browser data does not remove the backups, so delete them on the ComfyUI side yourself when you no longer need them. If backup is not enabled, data lost from your browser cannot be restored. Keep important chats by exporting them and saving them on your device, or by backing them up to a ComfyUI that only you use. As described above, backups on a shared server may be readable by others.

6. Delivery logs
The App's files are delivered by Cloudflare, Inc. ("Cloudflare"). For delivery and to protect against abuse, Cloudflare logs IP addresses, access times, requested URLs, browser types and similar data. We do not look at individual log entries; we only look at the counts Cloudflare aggregates (such as the number of requests and requests per country) to understand how much the App is used and to protect its delivery. This processing is based on our legitimate interests. Because we look only at aggregates and not at individual entries, because the logs are essential for delivery and protection against abuse, and because such logs are within what users of web services normally expect, we consider that these interests do not override your rights and interests. Logs may be handled outside the EU, including in the United States; Cloudflare safeguards these transfers through the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses. How long logs are kept is determined by Cloudflare, and we cannot change it (please see Cloudflare's privacy policy). The aggregates we look at remain only for the period they can be viewed in Cloudflare's dashboard ([period]). Users in the EU and elsewhere have, as provided by law, the right to access, rectify and erase their information, to restrict and object to its processing, and to data portability, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

7. API keys
The App keeps the API keys you enter only while the page is open and does not store them in your browser. Keys are used only when sending requests to the Destinations you specify. However, keys may be read by other programs running in your browser, such as malicious extensions. If you want to save a key, please use your browser's password manager (its handling follows your browser's provider). In case a key leaks, we recommend using keys with an expiry date or a spending limit.

8. Security
For information we receive through inquiries and reports, we take measures to prevent leaks, loss and damage, such as limiting who can see it to ourselves and the contractors we entrust with operations (such as Cloudflare, which handles the form and the email forwarding). If you ask through the contact in 11, we will tell you what these measures are. However, we cannot guarantee that sending and receiving data over the internet is completely secure.
For the parts of the App that handle API keys and chats, the main measures we take include the following:
- LLM responses are shown as text and are not interpreted as HTML.
- Images in responses are not loaded; they are shown as links.
- Only http and https links can be opened.
- Our delivery settings allow only programs delivered from our domain to run.
- In browsers that support it, inserting strings as HTML or programs is limited to fixed forms (Trusted Types).
When we learn of a vulnerability, we endeavor to deliver a fix. Even so, we cannot remove every risk. Do not put information you could not afford to have leaked into chats with web search enabled. If we learn of an incident, we will endeavor to announce it in the App's repository (GitHub) and to explain the necessary steps, such as revoking keys. We accept vulnerability reports through GitHub private vulnerability reporting.

9. Requests regarding your information
For information about you that we received through inquiries or reports, you may, as provided by law, ask us to notify you of its purpose of use, to disclose, correct, add to or delete it, to stop using or erase it, or to stop providing it to third parties. Please contact us through the form in 11. We will confirm that you are the person concerned and respond in accordance with the law. We do not hold the information kept in your browser (see 2), so please delete it yourself from your browser settings.

10. Changes to this policy
When we change this policy, we will notify you on the App's screen or in its repository (GitHub) of the new content and the date it takes effect, before that date. For important changes that are unfavorable to you, we will ask for your agreement again on the App's screen.

11. Contact
We also accept bug reports and feature requests in GitHub Issues in the App's repository. It is public, so do not write personal information, API keys or the URLs of your servers there. For general questions, inquiries involving personal information, the requests in 9 and reports under Article 7 of the Terms of Use, please use the contact form (https://contact.kisara-ui.com). Enter an email address at which you can receive our reply. What you enter in the form is received by Cloudflare Workers and reaches us as an email through Cloudflare's email forwarding (Email Routing). To limit repeated submissions, the sender's IP address is counted within Cloudflare for a short time only; it is not stored with the content. We use the contact details and content we receive only to respond to inquiries and reports, and delete them once we have finished handling them and no longer need them.
You can attach up to 3 files to the form (a diagnostics file, screen images in PNG, JPEG or WebP, and text files; up to 5 MB each and 10 MB in total). A diagnostics file is a file for investigating problems that you export from the App's settings. It contains the errors that occurred (message, where they occurred and stack trace) and a record of the operations before and after them, the versions of the App, your browser and the connected ComfyUI, the kind of device you use, and the settings chosen from options. It does not contain chats, prompts, workflow values, images or API keys. The URLs of the destinations (ComfyUI and LLM) are included by default, and you can remove them before exporting. You can check its full content before exporting it. Screen images contain whatever is shown in them, such as chats and generated images, so please check them before attaching them. Like the content of an inquiry, attached files are used only to investigate problems and to respond to the inquiry, and are deleted once we have finished handling them and no longer need them.
Controller of your information: Usegrid institute (contact: the form in section 11). Matters that the Act on the Protection of Personal Information of Japan requires us to make available to the data subject are handled, within the scope the law requires, only at the request of a person whose information we have received, after verifying that person's identity.

12. Language
This policy is written in Japanese. Versions in other languages are translations for reference, and if they differ, the Japanese version prevails.
